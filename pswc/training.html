Training

Programmes

Three routes through the same system. Every one of them starts with an evaluation, and every one of them is built in the same order: engagement, clarity, foundations, then finish. Nothing is skipped because a client is in a hurry.

Sport

For competition-track dogs and their handlers

Development for dogs headed toward Mondioring, IGP or protection sport. The emphasis is on building a dog that works because it is clear and committed, not because it has been wound up. Handlers are trained alongside the dog — a sport dog is a two-animal problem.

  • Engagement and reward mechanics
  • Position work and precision obedience
  • Drive development and channelling
  • Grip development and equipment introduction
  • Trial routine and competition foundations
Delivery: weekly sessions, group work, or board-and-train — format and pricing to confirm

Working

For detection, task and deployment-track dogs

Dogs that are expected to perform in an environment nobody rehearsed. The programme prioritises independent problem-solving, environmental neutrality and a search behaviour the handler can actually read — because a detection dog is only as useful as the handler's ability to call the change of behaviour.

  • Odour imprinting and source commitment
  • Search patterns and handler mechanics
  • Environmental confidence on unfamiliar surfaces and heights
  • Distraction and noise proofing
  • Task development and duration building
Suitable for handler teams and prospect assessment — scope to confirm

Companion

For pet owners who want a dog that is easy to live with

The largest group of dogs PSWC sees, and the one most often let down by training that is either too soft to work or too heavy for the job. Companion clients get the same foundations as the sport dogs, aimed at a different outcome: a dog that settles in the house, walks without a fight, and comes back when it is called.

  • Puppy development and early socialisation
  • Loose-lead walking and door manners
  • Recall under real distraction
  • Settle, place and duration work
  • Problem behaviour: reactivity, jumping, resource guarding
Private sessions and structured packages — format and pricing to confirm
How it runs

What to expect

No programme is quoted before the dog has been seen. The evaluation is not a sales meeting — it is the first training session, and it decides what the plan is.

Owners are expected to be present and to train. PSWC does not hand back a finished dog and wish you luck; the handover is part of the programme, because the dog will go home to the person who has to maintain it.

  • 01 Evaluation & goal setting
  • 02 Written training plan
  • 03 Foundation phase
  • 04 Proofing & generalisation
  • 05 Handler transfer
  • 06 Follow-up & maintenance
