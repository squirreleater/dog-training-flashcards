Why this page exists

Most training websites make claims. This one keeps a record.

Vesi is being developed on the PSWC system from puppy foundations through to Mondioring, and each stage is documented as it happens — including the parts that had to be repeated. If you want to know how a dog would be trained here, this is more useful than a paragraph about philosophy.

Entries are added as the work is done. Nothing is written retrospectively to look tidier than it was.

Development record

Six stages

01 Weeks 8–12

Puppy Foundations

Engagement before obedience. The first weeks are spent making the handler the most interesting thing in a neutral room, then a mildly interesting one — food and toy mechanics, marker conditioning, and a clean reward event the puppy can predict.

No commands are formally trained in this stage. Position work is shaped and paid, but nothing is named until the behaviour is reliable enough to deserve a cue.

Complete
02 Months 3–5

Environmental Development

Graded exposure with the choice left to the dog. Surfaces, heights, sound, footing, crowds and vehicles — introduced one variable at a time, at a distance the puppy can think at, and left before she is tired of it.

The measure of success is not that she tolerates the environment. It is how fast she recovers when something goes wrong, and whether she offers engagement afterwards without being asked.

Complete
03 Months 5–9

Obedience

Formal positions, duration and the beginnings of precision. Cues are attached to behaviours that already exist. Duration is built in low-distraction settings first, then generalised deliberately — new place, same criteria, lower expectation.

This is also where handler mechanics get tested. Most obedience problems at this stage are timing problems, not dog problems.

In progress
04 Planned

Drive Development

Building the intensity and the off-switch at the same time. Prey work with clean rules, controlled outs, and the conditioning that lets a dog stay in a high-arousal state without losing access to trained behaviour.

Upcoming
05 Planned

Sport Foundations

Equipment introduction, grip development, and the specific exercise components of the sport — trained separately and to a standard before they are ever chained into a routine.

Upcoming
06 Planned

Mondioring

Routine assembly, decoy work, and the variability the sport is built around. Mondioring rewards a dog that can think under pressure and a handler who has prepared for a scenario they have not seen before — which is the whole point.
